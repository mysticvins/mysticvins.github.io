Questions

Before your first session

I've never had a reading. Is that okay?

Completely. Many people who come to us are first-timers. You don't need to know anything about tarot or energy healing; just bring the question or feeling that's on your mind.

How do online sessions work?

You meet Vinita on a video or phone call at a time that suits you. We'll share the details on WhatsApp once your slot is confirmed.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm the session, time and price on WhatsApp, you pay by UPI to hold your slot.

Is what I share kept private?

Yes. Every session is personal and confidential.

Can a session replace medical or professional advice?

No. Sessions support wellbeing and personal growth. They don't replace medical, legal or financial advice, and no outcome is ever promised.

Which service should I choose?

Try the 30-second session finder, or choose "Not sure, please suggest" in the enquiry form and we'll guide you.
